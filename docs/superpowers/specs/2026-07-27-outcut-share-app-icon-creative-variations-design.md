# Outcut Share Creative App Icon Variations

Date: 2026-07-27

## Goal

Add five original macOS app-icon concepts beside the existing six without
changing or deleting the existing artwork.

The new concepts communicate Outcut Share's ability to extract an arbitrary
screen region and share it as a separate destination. The six supplied
inspiration images are not used as generation inputs for this batch. Their
specific glyphs, compositions, and palettes must not be reproduced.

## Direction

The batch favors semantic abstraction over literal monitors, window chrome,
crop brackets, stacked panes, or cursor-on-screen compositions.

Every concept must:

- use a distinct metaphor, silhouette, and dominant palette;
- remain front-facing and legible at 64 × 64 pixels;
- use a compact rounded-square macOS enclosure;
- contain no text, letters, numbers, trademarks, or watermarks;
- avoid isometric scenes, product renders, pedestals, and floor shadows;
- avoid duplicating any of the six existing icon concepts;
- be delivered as a flattened 1024 × 1024 PNG.

## Concepts

### 07 Portal Cut

A deep cobalt enclosure holds a soft coral disc with one crisp rectangular
opening punched through it. A small detached tile emerging at the right edge
suggests the selected region becoming independently shareable.

### 08 Relay Ribbon

A dark teal enclosure contains one continuous mint-to-aqua ribbon. The ribbon
loops around a source rectangle, crosses through a narrow bridge, and resolves
into a smaller destination tile. It communicates transfer without arrows,
screens, or window chrome.

### 09 Prism Slice

A near-black plum enclosure contains a diagonal translucent prism. A bright
rectangular slice within the prism remains straight and sharply bounded while
the surrounding color splits into cyan, violet, and warm pink.

### 10 Magnetic Crop

A pale stone enclosure contains four asymmetric rounded blocks in vermilion,
ultramarine, chartreuse, and violet. They are pulled toward—but never touch—a
clean rectangular negative space in the center.

### 11 Broadcast Aperture

A deep forest enclosure contains one asymmetric ivory aperture with a vivid
lime selected region. Two restrained signal arcs emerge only from the selected
region's right edge, making the sharing action secondary to the aperture.
